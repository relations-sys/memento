    The façade

    Five moves for a Memento façade.

    • 01 · MaterialAn ink-black façade

      A dark, matte skin. The building reads as one block, an object set down in the street.

    • 02 · WindowsEvery window is a door

      A concrete frame, open at the bottom, surrounds each window: the brand symbol, repeated over thirteen floors. At the very top right, a single vermilion frame.

    • 03 · SignThe vertical line

      The stair tower carries “HÔTEL · MEMENTO · MONTRÉAL” in tall letters, like a laneway sign.

    • 04 · Ground floorA wide-open café

      The storefront runs the full width and the entrance takes the shape of the door. A blade sign marks the hotel at street level.

    • 05 · RoofA glass pavilion

      The mezzanine becomes a set-back lantern that lights up above the city at night.
